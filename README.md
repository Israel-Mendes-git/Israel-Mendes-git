# Israel Mendes

**Desenvolvedor de jogos e ferramentas** · Cascavel, Ceará 🇧🇷

Trabalho na [Rapadura Atômica](https://rapaduraatomica.com.br), estúdio de animação e jogos
digitais, em três frentes que raramente aparecem no mesmo cargo: **C++** no software de
animação do estúdio, **web em produção** e **ferramentas de pipeline** em Python.
Fora do expediente, faço jogo — roguelike e sistema de cartas, principalmente.

O que me interessa é o momento em que uma regra vira sensação: por que uma carta parece
pesada, por que uma morte dói, por que uma ferramenta some do caminho de quem a usa.

**Game and tools developer**, based in Cascavel, Ceará, Brazil.

I work at [Rapadura Atômica](https://rapaduraatomica.com.br), an animation and games studio,
across three fronts that rarely sit in the same role: **C++** in the studio's animation
software, **web apps in production**, and **Python pipeline tooling**. After hours I make
games — roguelikes and card systems, mostly.

What interests me is the moment a rule turns into a feeling: why a card feels heavy, why a
death stings, why a tool disappears from the way of whoever uses it.

**In production**

- **[Nuclear](https://github.com/Rapadura-Atomica/Nuclear)** — a C++ Blender fork that became
  the studio's 2D animation software. I am the project's second developer, with 203 commits.
  It animates real series, every day.
- **[Filmerama](https://github.com/Israel-Mendes-git/Rapadura_filmes)** — the studio's
  streaming platform, live at [filmerama.com](https://filmerama.com). React.
- **[UI Kanban](https://github.com/Israel-Mendes-git/Uikanban)** — an Android TV Kanboard
  client in Kotlin, putting the team board on a production-room screen.

**Games**

- **[Guilda da Corrupção](https://github.com/Israel-Mendes-git/Guilda-da-Corrupcao)** — a
  guild-management roguelike with card combat. Playable end to end, with a balance simulator
  running the real combat rules and a 38-check smoke test covering a whole run. Unity · C#
- **[O Grito da Mata](https://github.com/Israel-Mendes-git/Roguelike)** — a roguelike with
  procedural map generation built on graphs and maze algorithms. Unity · C#
- **[Dizido](https://github.com/Israel-Mendes-git/Dizido)** — team chat where the decision
  outlives the conversation. .NET 10 · Blazor · SignalR
